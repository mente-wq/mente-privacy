4. Sensitive and Health-Related Information

Some MENTE features can involve information that may reveal aspects of a user's mental wellbeing or health-related circumstances. Examples include mood information, self-reflections, assessment responses, wellness activity, and crisis/help interactions that you choose to use.

We treat this information as sensitive and use it only to provide the requested app functionality, maintain security and safety, support moderation where applicable, and comply with legal obligations.

Please do not enter information into MENTE that you do not want stored as part of the feature you are using. In an immediate medical or safety emergency, contact local emergency services rather than relying on the app.

5. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain your MENTE account and application features.
  • Provide personalized wellness, growth, mood, assessment, support-tool, and progress functionality.
  • Save favorites, history, completion records, and other information when you request those features.
  • Provide community messaging, posts, comments, reactions, reporting, and moderation.
  • Operate authentication, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and service reliability functions.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or account-deletion requests.
  • Comply with applicable law, legal process, and legitimate safety requirements.

MENTE does not use sensitive wellness or health-related information for unrelated advertising profiling or to make employment, insurance, credit, housing, or similar eligibility decisions.

6. AI and Automated Features

Some MENTE features may use AI-powered services to generate personalized wellness or growth content based on information you choose to provide. Where an AI service is involved, only information needed for the requested feature should be sent to that service.

AI-generated content is not a diagnosis, medical judgment, emergency response, or replacement for a qualified healthcare professional. You should not rely on AI output as the sole basis for urgent medical or mental-health decisions.

7. Third-Party Services

MENTE may rely on third-party service providers to operate parts of the application. These may include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, backend services, and application data processing.
  • YouTube / Google services when you choose to view embedded wellness videos or related video content.
  • AI service providers when you explicitly use an AI-powered MENTE feature.
  • External APIs or content services used for specific wellness or exercise features.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual terms. MENTE does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

8. Sharing of Information

MENTE does not sell your personal or sensitive information.

Information may be shared or made available only where reasonably necessary to operate the requested feature, including:

  • Service providers that host or process data on MENTE's behalf.
  • Other community users when you intentionally publish information in a community, post, message, comment, or other public/shared area.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to address serious security, fraud, abuse, or safety concerns.
  • Third parties you intentionally interact with through a feature, such as external video/content providers.

Private wellness entries and account information are not intentionally published to other users merely because you use MENTE.

9. Community Privacy

Information you deliberately post in MENTE Communities should be treated as shared information. Messages, posts, comments, reactions, and other community activity may be visible to members of the relevant community or channel according to the feature's design.

Do not share private medical records, passwords, financial credentials, home addresses, or other highly sensitive information in public or group community areas.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and device secure and for notifying us of suspected unauthorized access.

11. Data Retention

MENTE retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the feature for which it was collected, maintain account and service functionality, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security.

Retention periods can differ by data type. Some technical records or records required by law may need to be retained for a limited period after account deletion where legally permitted or required.

12. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your MENTE account and associated personal data from inside the app or through the public account-deletion page:

Request MENTE account deletion

When an account deletion request is completed, MENTE will delete or de-identify the account data and user-associated application data that is within MENTE's control, subject to limited exceptions described below.

Data normally deleted

  • Account/profile records associated with your MENTE account.
  • Mood, wellness, growth, support-tool, and progress records associated with the account.
  • Favorites and saved application records.
  • Community membership and user-generated content, subject to the deletion/anonymization behavior implemented for the specific community feature.
  • Assessment attempts and responses associated with the account.

Possible limited retention

Some information may be retained for a limited period when required by law, necessary to establish or defend legal claims, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, or held by an independent third-party provider under that provider's own legally applicable retention rules. Where retention is required, the retained information will be restricted and used only for the permitted purpose.

Deleting the MENTE account is not the same as uninstalling the application. Uninstalling the app does not by itself request account deletion.

Account Deletion

Use this section to request deletion of your MENTE account and associated personal data.

Before you delete your account

Account deletion is permanent. Deleting your account can remove access to your MENTE profile, saved items, mood and wellness history, growth information, assessments, support-tool history, community membership, and other account-associated data.

Uninstalling MENTE does not delete your account. You must use the in-app deletion option or the request method described below.

Request deletion

  1. Use the in-app option first. Open MENTE → Account/Profile → Settings → Delete Account, where available.
  2. For the web request, contact MENTE using the privacy email below. Use the same email address associated with your MENTE account so that the request can be verified.
  3. Use the subject “MENTE Account Deletion Request”. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive information in the request.
  4. Follow any identity-verification instructions. We may need to verify that the requester controls the account before deleting it.

Request account deletion by email

Privacy / deletion email: [REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-PRIVACY-EMAIL]

What happens after a request

After the deletion request is verified, MENTE will process deletion of account-associated information within its control. Information that must be retained for a legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legally permitted purpose may be retained for the limited period necessary for that purpose.

Where deletion of a specific community record requires preservation of community integrity or moderation history, the record may be deleted, de-identified, or otherwise handled according to the applicable retention requirement and MENTE's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services may have their own independent retention policies for information they process. MENTE's deletion process will address information under MENTE's control and applicable provider controls.
